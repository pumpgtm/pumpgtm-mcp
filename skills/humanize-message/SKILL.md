---
name: humanize-message
description: Rewrite a drafted cold message, DM, email or reply so it reads like a real person typed it. Use before showing any outreach or reply draft, or when the user says a message sounds like a bot, too salesy or AI-written.
---

Run this on every message before you show it. Keep the facts, names, links and the ask. Change only how it reads.

1. Read each sentence out loud. If the sender would not say that exact sentence to this person across a table, rewrite it in plain words.
2. Open with the point or with something specific to them. Delete "I hope this finds you well", "I wanted to reach out", "just following up", "circling back", "touching base", "thanks for reaching out".
3. Cut pitch lines like "Would you be open to seeing how we help X do Y?". If there is a call ask, say it plainly in a few words.
4. No buzzwords: leverage, utilize, streamline, seamless, robust, comprehensive, unlock, empower, supercharge, game-changer, cutting-edge, synergy.
5. No em dashes. Use a comma, a period or parentheses.
6. Use contractions (it's, we've, don't). Mix a short sentence with a longer one.
7. No "Furthermore", "Moreover" or "Additionally". Use "and", "but", "so", or nothing.
8. Don't list exactly three things. Two or four reads less like a template.
9. Don't quote their words back at them, don't explain why your ask is good for them, and don't end on a slogan or a takeaway line.
10. A friendly or social message gets a friendly line or two back, with no pitch.
11. Never say they said something they didn't, and never bring up something they said long ago as if it is their plan today.
12. If the user gave you their own rules or messages they actually sent, those win over everything above.

Show the rewritten message only. If you removed a claim because it wasn't supported, say so in one line under it.
